About Us

At Lower Alabama Custom Firearms, we combine a deep respect for traditional craftsmanship with modern precision. Serving firearm enthusiasts, collectors, and those preserving family history, we take pride in offering professional-grade sales and specialized services tailored to your exact standards.

Whether you are looking to acquire a new firearm, coordinate a legal transfer, or commission a personalized piece, our work is defined by meticulous attention to detail. Our core specialties include:

Heirloom-Quality Restoration: Breathing new life into vintage, worn, or inherited pieces so they can be safely enjoyed and passed down for generations.

Custom Laser Engraving & Plating: Elevating firearms into true works of art with precision engraving and high-end finishes, including professional high-polish nickel and 24K gold plating.

New Custom Builds: Crafting custom configurations tailored entirely to your preferences.

Professional Sales & Transfers: Delivering trusted, fully compliant FFL sales, shipping, and background check services.

Built on a foundation of quality, integrity, and local craftsmanship, Lower Alabama Custom Firearms is dedicated to treating every piece that comes through our doors with the utmost care and respect.

New Firearms Sales 

Lower Alabama Custom Firearms offers retail sales for brand-new firearms alongside tailored custom orders. Whether you are looking for a standard factory model or searching for a specific configuration, they provide access to quality inventory to meet your needs.

Restoration

Dedicated to bringing vintage, worn, or inherited pieces back to life, their restoration service focuses on heirloom-quality craftsmanship. This service carefully preserves or renews historical integrity and mechanical function so that family heirlooms and classic firearms can be passed down for generations.

Transfers And Shipping

Operating under official FFL licensing (License No. 1-63-097-01-7K-1032), they handle legal firearm transfers and shipping logistics. As noted on their site, all sales and services are fully compliant and subject to required background checks.

Custom Engraving & Plating

For personalized aesthetics and high-end finishes, they offer specialized custom laser engraving alongside professional-grade decorative finishing. This includes premium options such as high-polish nickel and 24K gold plating to give firearms a distinct, museum-grade appearance.
